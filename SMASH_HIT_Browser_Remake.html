SMASH HIT

A one-file technical fan remake. Move automatically through five fixed checkpoints. Aim with the pointer and tap once to launch one shot.

Shatter crystals to gain balls. Ten consecutive crystals increase multiball; pass a crystal or collide and the streak is lost. Break local holes through glass instead of wasting ammunition on the whole pane.

Desktop: mouse to aim, click to fire, P or Escape to pause.
Mobile: drag to aim, quick tap to fire.

This independently written fan project uses no commercial game art, code, models, textures, fonts, or sound files. SMASH HIT is referenced only to identify the game being remade.
